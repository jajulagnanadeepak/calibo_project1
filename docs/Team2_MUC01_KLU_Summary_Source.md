# TEAM 2 — RETAIL PULSE
## Category Intelligence Executive Summary

**Project:** MUC01 — KLU Retail Sales Analysis  
**Team:** Team 2  
**Analysis Period:** January–June 2026

---

## 1. Three Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Category Revenue Trend
**Electronics** generated the highest category revenue at **₹226.53M**, but it also recorded the **steepest decline of 35.02%** from January to June 2026. This makes Electronics the highest-priority category for further investigation.

### Finding 2 — Category & Product/Supplier Review
Electronics is the strongest revenue contributor, so its declining trend should be examined at product and supplier level. Weak-performing products should be reviewed for possible issues involving pricing, demand, availability, or supplier performance.

### Finding 3 — Discount Effectiveness
Transactions with **0% discount** generated **₹4,685.65 average revenue per transaction**, compared with **₹4,052.28** for **25%+ discount**. Average units sold were almost identical (**2.59 vs 2.58**). This suggests that higher discounts did not provide a proportionate increase in sales volume.

---

## 2. Category Recommendation

**Prioritize Electronics for management attention and supplier review.** Its high revenue contribution combined with a 35.02% January-to-June decline creates the strongest business risk.

Maintain stable categories such as **Grocery**, which showed the most consistent monthly revenue performance. Review weaker products and use targeted promotions rather than broad high-discount strategies.

The strongest revenue period was **January (₹84.77M)**, while **June was lowest (₹69.28M)**. Saturday was the strongest revenue day (**₹91.61M**), suggesting that inventory and promotional planning should consider higher-demand periods.

---

## 3. Limitation

The analysis is based primarily on **revenue, transactions, units sold, category, product, discount, date, and store information**. It does not include supplier cost, product margin, inventory availability, or promotion costs. Therefore, revenue performance alone cannot establish true profitability or the exact cause of category declines.

---

### Executive Takeaway

**Protect the revenue base while investigating the Electronics decline, use stable categories as benchmarks, and apply discounts selectively based on measurable sales response.**
